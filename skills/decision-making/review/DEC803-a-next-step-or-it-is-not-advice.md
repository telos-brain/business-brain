# A Next Step or It Is Not Advice

Insight that leaves the other person with nowhere to put their feet
is a performance. Advice is a next step they can take — today, or
on a named date — not a description of how the world is.

## Steps

1. State the counsel in one line.
2. Add the next step: who does what, by when, or what they will
   stop. If you cannot, you are not finished.
3. Check it is small enough to start (**DEC701**). A next step that
   is another strategy document is still a speech.

## Rules

- "Think about it" is not a next step.
- Several pages of analysis still need one move at the end.
- Distinct from **ADV502** (say less). This skill is whether the
  few words *point at an act*.

## Related skills

- **ADV701** — say what this is really about, then add the step
- **ADV502** — the step can be one line
- **DEC705** — the step can be to stop
