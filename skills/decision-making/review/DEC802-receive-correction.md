# Receive Correction

The wise person uses a wound. The fool writes a defence. If the
correction is specific and from someone who can see the work, your
first job is to understand it — not to survive it.

## Steps

1. Restate the correction in your own words until they agree you
   heard it.
2. Ask: if this were true, what would I change? Try that on before
   you rebut.
3. Keep the part that survives. File it (**DEC801**). Drop the
   theatre.

## Rules

- A vague attack is not a rebuke. Ask for the specific, or let it go.
- Explaining *why* you did it is usually a dodge. Explain what you
  will do next.
- Distinct from **ADV505** (whether *they* are teachable). This skill
  is whether *you* are.
