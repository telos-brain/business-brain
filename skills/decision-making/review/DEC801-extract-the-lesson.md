# Extract the Lesson

A short after-action: what we intended, what happened, why, what we
keep or change. The point is the next decision, not a verdict on the
last one.

## Steps

1. **Intend** — what did we mean to happen, and what did we decide?
2. **Happen** — what actually happened? Facts only.
3. **Why** — the one or two causes that matter. Do not write a history.
4. **Keep or change** — one practice to repeat, or one practice to
   drop. File the practice as an Insight (or a skill if it is
   transferable). Do not reopen the original choice unless the *same*
   choice is in front of you again.

## Rules

- Hindsight will make the outcome look obvious. Judge the decision
  against what was known *then*, not what you know now.
- A bad outcome can follow a good process. A good outcome can follow
  a sloppy one. Extract the process lesson, not the score.
- Relitigating ("we should never have…") is not a lesson unless it
  names a rule you will use next time.
