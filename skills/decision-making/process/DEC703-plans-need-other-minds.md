# Plans Need Other Minds

A plan made alone is a first draft. Other minds are not politics. They
are how you find the missing constraint before it finds you.

## Steps

1. State the plan in a few lines and name the door (**DEC701**).
2. If it is one-way, or wide blast radius (**DEC601**), you need at
   least one other competent mind who does not report to your hope.
3. Ask them to break it (**ADV601**) or to name the killer
   (**ADV602**). Use what they say. Do not collect nods.

## Rules

- Agreement from people who need you to be right is not counsel.
- Two-way doors can skip this. That is the point of a two-way door.
- Distinct from **DEC401** (information you have not retrieved). This
  skill is *judgment* you have not invited.
