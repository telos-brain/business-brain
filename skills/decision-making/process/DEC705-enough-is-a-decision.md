# Enough Is a Decision

Without a finish line, more feels like progress. It is often delay
with better manners. Enough is a decision you make *before* you are
tired of the work — what would count, then stop.

## Steps

1. Name what "enough" is for this decision or piece of work — a
   number, a test, a date, or a smallest useful version.
2. Write it where the team can see it.
3. When you hit it, desist. Further work needs a new decision, not
   momentum.

## Rules

- "A bit more" is not a criterion. If you cannot name the stop, you
  do not have a plan.
- Enough is not mediocrity. It is the point where more will not
  change the action (**DEC702**).
- Distinct from **DEC702** (this information cannot help). This
  skill is *you already have enough — quit adding*.
