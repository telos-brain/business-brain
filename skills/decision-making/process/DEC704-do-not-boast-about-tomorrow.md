# Do Not Boast About Tomorrow

You can plan the path. You cannot command the day. Certainty about a
future you do not own makes you brittle — you stop watching, and you
sell the ending before it happens.

## Steps

1. Rewrite the claim from "we will" to "we intend, if these conditions
   hold".
2. Name the two conditions most likely to fail.
3. Decide what you will do if they fail — a smaller bet, a review date,
   or a walk-back (**DEC701**).

## Rules

- Humility about outcomes is not an excuse to avoid deciding.
- Public boasts raise the cost of changing your mind. Prefer quiet
  commits on two-way doors.
- Distinct from **DEC702** (stop gathering). This skill is how you
  *talk* about the plan once you have decided.
