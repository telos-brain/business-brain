# Do Not Believe Every Report

A report is a claim with a sender. The simple treat it as the world.
The prudent ask who benefits, what was measured, and what step you
can check yourself.

## Steps

1. Name the claim and who brought it.
2. Ask: what would I see if this were true? Pick one check you can
   do (a number, a customer, a file, a person who was there).
3. Do that check, or mark the decision as provisional until you
   have. Do not build the next week on an unchecked story.

## Rules

- Urgency is how a bad report skips the check.
- Trusting a person is not the same as skipping their figures
  (**BUS403**).
- Distinct from **ADV203** (hear the other side of a dispute). This
  skill is *any* report that wants to become action.
