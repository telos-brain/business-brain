# Iron Sharpens Iron

You get sharper against a mind that will not flatter you. A room of
agreement feels like alignment. It is often rust. The gift is friction
with someone competent who wants the work to be good.

## Steps

1. Name who currently reviews the important calls.
2. If they never disagree, you do not have a review. You have an
   audience.
3. Put one person in the path who will break the plan (**DEC703**,
   **ADV601**) and who has no reason to need your mood.

## Rules

- Conflict without competence is just noise. Choose iron, not gravel.
- You must be able to take the spark (**DEC802**). Otherwise you will
  fire the sharp people.
- Distinct from **BUS201** (leave a bad table). This skill is *install
  a sharp one*.
