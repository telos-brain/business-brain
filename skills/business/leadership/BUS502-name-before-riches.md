# Name Before Riches

Money can be remade. A name, once spent, is expensive to buy back.
Deals that need you to become slightly smaller as a person are
mispriced, even when the cash is large.

## Steps

1. Name the upside in money or speed.
2. Name what the deal does to how you are trusted — by customers,
   the people who work with you, and anyone who has to reuse your
   word.
3. If the name gets cheaper, walk. If both can rise, proceed with
   honest weights (**BUS403**).

## Rules

- "Nobody will know" is how a name is spent.
- A good name is not branding. It is whether people can plan on you.
- Distinct from **BUS403** (the figures). This skill is the *asset
  those figures sit on*.
