# Do Not Partner Incompatible Aims

A partner is not extra hands. They are a second aim with a vote.
If your "done" and theirs cannot both be true, the project will
spend the name, the cash, and the calendar until someone pretends
it was a misunderstanding.

## Steps

1. Write your aim for this work in one line (**ADV803**).
2. Write theirs — not the slogan, what they would count as a win.
3. If the two cannot be true together, do not partner. Buy a
   service, hire for a bounded task, or walk. Do not paper it
   with a warm meeting.

## Rules

- Compatibility of manners is not compatibility of aims.
- A 50/50 split does not fix two destinations.
- Distinct from **ADV803** (pick *your* aim). This skill is
  *do not strap yourself to the other one*.
