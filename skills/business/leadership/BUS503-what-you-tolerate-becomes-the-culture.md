# What You Tolerate Becomes the Culture

People do not copy the speech. They copy what goes unchallenged.
A late report, a shaded number, a public slight — if it stands,
it is the standard, whatever the values page says.

## Steps

1. Name the behaviour you have allowed more than once.
2. Ask: if a new person copied only this, what shop would we have?
3. Either stop it in the open (a rule, a conversation, a
   consequence) or admit it is now the culture and stop pretending.

## Rules

- Silence is a policy. So is a laugh.
- One hard conversation is cheaper than a year of copies.
- Distinct from **BUS305** (the first small compromise). This
  skill is *your* ongoing permission.
