# Do Not Hire the Surface

A polished person can interview. Competence leaves a trail — work
you can look at, a problem they actually finished, someone who
used the result. Hire the trail, or you have hired a costume.

## Steps

1. Ignore the surface for a moment (fluency, pedigree, charm).
2. Ask for the work: a thing they made, a decision they owned, a
   customer or colleague who will speak to the *result*.
3. If you cannot see the work, do not hire the story. A trial on
   a two-way door (**DEC701**) beats a speech.

## Rules

- Confidence is not evidence. Neither is your hope.
- A short paid test is cheaper than a year of managing the
  costume.
- Distinct from **BUS201** (the table you sit at). This skill is
  *who you let in*.
