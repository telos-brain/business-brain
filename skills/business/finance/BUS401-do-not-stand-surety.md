# Do Not Stand Surety

A guarantee looks like loyalty. It is a transfer of their downside onto
your balance sheet — often without control of their decisions. If they
fail, you pay, and you did not get the upside.

## Steps

1. Name whose risk this is, and who controls the actions that create it.
2. Ask: if it goes wrong, can I pay without wrecking my own options
   (**BUS404**)?
3. If you cannot carry it, refuse. If you can, take it as a *priced*
   decision (equity, control, a cap) — not as a favour.

## Rules

- "It probably won't happen" is not a capacity test.
- Guaranteeing a process you do not run is the expensive form.
- Distinct from **DEC601** (blast radius of *your* decision). This
  skill is borrowing *their* blast radius.
