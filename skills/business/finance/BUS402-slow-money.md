# Slow Money

Wealth that arrives in a hurry usually has a hidden bill — leverage,
a fad, or someone else's greater fool. Slow money is boring on purpose.
It survives contact with a normal year.

## Steps

1. Name the promised speed and the instrument (debt, a tip, a shortcut,
   a once-only window).
2. Ask what has to stay true for the hurry to work. If the answer is
   "sentiment" or "we get out first", walk away.
3. Prefer the smaller, slower path that still compounds. Price the
   hurry as a one-way door (**DEC701**).

## Rules

- Fast is allowed when the door is two-way and the downside is named.
- "Everyone is doing it" is a reason to slow down, not speed up.
- Distinct from **DEC502** (what you forgo). This skill is *pace*.

## Related skills
