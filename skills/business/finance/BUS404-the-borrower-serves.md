# The Borrower Serves

Interest is the visible price. The real price is who you have to obey
when the month is tight — a lender, a timeline, a covenant. Options
you used to have become someone else's.

## Steps

1. Name the repayment in cash *and* in freedom (what you cannot do
   until it is paid).
2. Ask: if revenue dips for a season, who decides what we cut?
3. Borrow only when the use is a field (**BUS301**), the door can
   still close (**DEC701**), and default does not take the name
   (**BUS502**).

## Rules

- Cheap credit is not the same as spare capacity.
- A loan that funds a house on a bare field fails twice.
- Distinct from **BUS401** (their debt). This skill is *yours*.
