# Honest Weights

A short measure today is a long debt tomorrow. People remember the
scale, not the excuse. Once they have to check your figures, every
later conversation is more expensive.

## Steps

1. Separate facts, estimates, and hopes. Label them as such.
2. Put the downside in the same units as the upside. Do not hide it
   in a footnote or a vibe.
3. If a number only works when you squint, change the decision — do
   not change the number.

## Rules

- Silence about a known risk is a dishonest weight.
- Rounding in your favour, once, is how the habit starts.
- Distinct from **ADV504** (say the hard thing). This skill is *do
  not cook the evidence*.
