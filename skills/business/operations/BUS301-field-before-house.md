# Field Before House

Expansion on a neglected field is theatre. The harvest you already
planted is the cheap win. The house can wait until the ground you have
is producing.

## Steps

1. List work already in the ground (customers, delivery, cash cycle,
   a half-finished system).
2. List the proposed house (new market, product, hire, rebuild).
3. Ask: will the house steal the labour the field still needs? If yes,
   finish or cut the field first (**ADV801**). Only then build.

## Rules

- "We can do both" fails unless spare capacity is named.
- A house that *is* the field (the next necessary step) is not this
  mistake. This skill catches decoration and empire.
- Distinct from **DEC502** (the next-best use). This skill is *order*.
