# Do Not Rewrite the Deal

A handshake is a line. Moving it in private — extra work, a new
date, a "small" cut — is a different deal that only one side signed.
That is how names get cheap (**BUS502**).

## Steps

1. Write what was agreed: deliverable, price, date, who does what.
2. Write what is now happening. If they differ, stop calling it the
   same deal.
3. Either honour the line, or go back and make a *new* handshake
   in the open. Do not slide.

## Rules

- Drift that "helps the customer" without saying so is still a
  rewrite. So is drift that helps you.
- If the original deal was foolish, renegotiate. Do not steal the
  difference.
- Distinct from **ADV508** (mean the word you say). This skill is
  the *line you already said together*.
