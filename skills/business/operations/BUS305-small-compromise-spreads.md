# Small Compromise Spreads

A small shade does not stay small. People copy what worked, not what
you meant. One exception becomes the method. Then you are surprised
that the culture matches the shortcut.

## Steps

1. Name the exception (the rounded figure, the skipped check, the
   "just this once").
2. Ask: if everyone did this next month, would I still call it
   small?
3. If no, refuse it, or make it an explicit, bounded rule — not a
   wink. If yes, it was never a compromise.

## Rules

- Intent does not contain the spread. The copy does.
- "Nobody will notice" is how a little shade becomes the standard
  (**BUS403**, **BUS503**).
- Distinct from **BUS503** (what the top allows). This skill is
  the *first* small move that will be copied.
