# Know the Actual State

A shop dies on a story it liked. Cash, capacity, and quality have
a current number. If you cannot say those numbers, you are advising
from a mood.

## Steps

1. Pick the three facts that would change this decision (cash,
   load, defect, a customer's actual status).
2. Get the current figure — not last month's, not the forecast.
3. Put the figure next to the story. If they disagree, the figure
   wins until you have checked it twice (**BUS202**).

## Rules

- "I think we are fine" is not a state.
- Looking once is cheaper than a quarter spent on the wrong plan.
- Distinct from **DEC401** (what you have not retrieved). This
  skill is the *operating* picture you should already own.

## Related skills

- **BUS202** — do not take the report as the state
- **BUS403** — do not shade the figure once you have it
- **DEC401** — if you truly do not have it, map the hole
