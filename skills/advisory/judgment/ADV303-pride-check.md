# Pride Check

Overconfidence feels like clarity. It usually means you have stopped
searching. The fall comes after the moment you decided nobody else had
anything to add.

## Steps

1. Notice the tell: "obviously", "anyone can see", no remaining
   questions, an audience that only agrees.
2. Run one forcing function — **DEC102** or **DEC703**. You need
   another mind or another explanation, not more of your own.
3. If you cannot name a way you might be wrong, you are not ready to
   lock a one-way door (**DEC701**).

## Rules

- Confidence after a search is earned. Confidence instead of a search
  is pride.
- Do not perform humility ("I might be wrong") and then skip the check.
- Distinct from **DEC301** (test a specific assumption). This skill
  catches the mood that skips testing altogether.
