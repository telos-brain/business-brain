# Check Your Own Fault First

Correction that skips your own part is theatre. If you helped make the
mess — a vague brief, a late call, a shaded number — start there. The
other person can hear a wound they did not have to carry alone.

## Steps

1. Name the fault you are about to point at.
2. Ask: what did *I* do, omit, or allow that made this easier?
3. Say that part first, in one line. Then give the correction
   (**ADV504**).
4. If you have no part, say so briefly and still keep the tone low
   (**ADV503**). Do not invent a confession.

## Rules

- Owning a share is not taking the whole blame.
- "We all make mistakes" is not a check. Name the specific act.
- Distinct from **DEC802** (receiving a wound). This skill is *before
  you give one*.
