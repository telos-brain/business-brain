# Stay Out of the Quarrel

A quarrel looks like a request for judgment. It is often a request for
an ally. Stepping in makes their conflict yours, and you inherit both
the heat and the aftermath.

## Steps

1. Ask: is this *my* decision, or am I being recruited?
2. If recruited, refuse the side. You may still name the pattern
   ("this is a fight, not a choice") and the cost of continuing.
3. If it *is* your decision, bound the ask (**ADV201**) and drop the
   people-drama from the frame.

## Rules

- Listening is not taking a side. Advising "stop" is not taking a side.
  Drafting their next attack *is*.
- Do not mediate unless both parties asked and you have standing.
- Distinct from **ADV505** (whether the person in front of you is
  teachable). Here the question is whether the *fight* is yours.
