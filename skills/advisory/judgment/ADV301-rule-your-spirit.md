# Rule Your Spirit

The first impulse is usually defence, attack, or speed. None of those is
judgment. If you cannot hold the reply, you do not have a position — the
position has you.

## Steps

1. Name the heat in one word (anger, fear, embarrassment, hurry).
2. Do not send, decide, or advise in that state. Wait until you can
   restate the other side fairly (**ADV501**).
3. Then give the smallest useful response — not the one that would feel
   satisfying.

## Rules

- Speed is not the same as courage. A pause is often the skilled move.
- If the window is genuinely closing, shrink the decision (**DEC701**)
  rather than venting into it.
- Distinct from **ADV503** (how you speak once you are calm). This skill
  is whether you speak yet.

## Related skills
