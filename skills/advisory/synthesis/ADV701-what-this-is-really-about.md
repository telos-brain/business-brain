# What This Is Really About

Synthesis is not a recap. It is the read that makes the rest of the
advice shorter.

## Steps

1. Ignore the order of the brief. What class of situation is this?
   (A priority fight, a reversible experiment, a diagnosis, a people
   problem dressed as a process problem, a one-way commitment.)
2. Write **one to three sentences**. If you need a fourth, you do not
   have it yet.
3. Check altitude. Too low: you have restated the facts. Too high: you
   could have said it about any problem. The test: would this sentence
   change what a good advisor does next?

## Rules

- No preamble. No "in summary". The synthesis *is* the opening.
- Do not smuggle the recommendation into the synthesis. Name the
  situation first; advise second.
- If you cannot say it in three lines, bound the ask (**ADV201**) —
  you are probably holding two problems.
