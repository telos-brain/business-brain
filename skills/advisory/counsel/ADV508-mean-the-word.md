# Mean the Word

A word that does not bind is noise. People plan on what you said, not
on what you meant to leave yourself. Soft yeses and elastic dates
spend the name (**BUS502**).

## Steps

1. Find every yes, date, and "we will" in the advice or the plan.
2. For each, ask: will I (or they) actually do this if the week goes
   badly?
3. Keep only the commitments you can keep. Change the rest to "I
   intend", "if X holds", or no.

## Rules

- Silence is cleaner than a yes you will walk back.
- A hedge that hides a no is a lie with manners.
- Distinct from **DEC704** (do not boast about tomorrow). This skill
  is the *sentence you are uttering now*.
