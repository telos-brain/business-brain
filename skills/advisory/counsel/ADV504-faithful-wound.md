# Faithful Wound

A friend will bruise you to spare you a worse injury. An enemy, or a
coward, will praise you into the wall. Counsel that never stings is
usually selling something — comfort, access, or the next engagement.

## Steps

1. Steelman the case (**ADV501**). If it still stands, do not invent a
   wound.
2. If there is a real gap, name it in one or two sentences. Be specific
   enough that they can act.
3. Leave the work with them. Do not rewrite their plan to prove you
   cared.

## Rules

- The test: would a friend thank you *later*, not in the room?
- Flattery and cruelty are both unfaithful. One hides the gap; the
  other enjoys it.
- Distinct from **ADV601** (how the plan fails). This skill is the
  duty to say it.
