# Take It to Them

A room will take a side. The person can still use a wound. What you
say about them when they are absent is usually for your comfort, not
their improvement.

## Steps

1. Name who the issue is actually with.
2. Ask: have I said this *to them*, specifically, without an audience?
3. If not, do that first — or advise the asker to. Do not draft the
   public version.
4. If they have already been told and will not hear it, **ADV505**.
   The room is still not the next step.

## Rules

- A witness is not the same as a crowd. One other mind can be counsel
  (**DEC703**). A performance is not.
- "I was just venting" is how a name gets spent (**BUS502**).
- Distinct from **ADV504** (the wound itself). This skill is *where
  you give it*.
