# Hold Your Tongue

Restraint is a form of respect. Extra sentences often exist to protect
the speaker, not to help the hearer. If it can be said in three lines,
the fourth is noise.

## Steps

1. Write the advice you want to give.
2. Cut every sentence that does not change what they should do or
   consider. Recap, hedging, and autobiography go first.
3. Stop. Do not add a paragraph to sound thorough.

## Rules

- Silence is allowed. "I have nothing useful to add" is counsel.
- Do not confuse brevity with vagueness. A short line still has to be
  specific.
- Distinct from **ADV701** (the read of the situation) and **ADV503**
  (tone). This skill is volume.
