# Who You Are Talking To

The same truth lands differently on a person who wants to get better
and a person who wants to win the exchange. Wisdom is not only what
you say. It is whether saying more will do any good.

## Steps

1. Sort the asker, lightly:
   - **Teachable** — they can restate a challenge without flinching.
   - **Performing** — they want approval or an audience.
   - **Scoffing** — they want you to fail in public.
2. Teachable: give the real counsel (**ADV504**).
3. Performing or scoffing: one short line, or none. Do not run the
   full method.

## Rules

- Do not diagnose character from one blunt question. Look at whether
  they use what you already said.
- A scoffer can become teachable later. You are deciding this *turn*,
  not their life.
- Distinct from **ADV201** (what the decision is). This skill is
  whether the *person* will use the advice.
