# Soft Answer

A hard truth can be said in a low voice. A soft truth said with contempt
still starts a fight. The goal is that they can *hear* it, not that you
are proven sharp.

## Steps

1. If you are hot, **ADV301** first. Do not soften a sentence you are
   still enjoying.
2. Lead with the steelman (**ADV501**) or the shared aim.
3. Put the gap in plain words, without heat, sarcasm, or a crowd.

## Rules

- Soft is not false. Do not hide the point to keep the peace.
- Do not sandwich so much praise around the gap that the gap vanishes.
- Distinct from **ADV504** (whether to wound at all). This skill is the
  temperature of the sentence.

## Related skills

- **ADV301** — rule your spirit before you answer
- **ADV501** — state their case fairly first
- **ADV504** — the wound still has to be faithful
