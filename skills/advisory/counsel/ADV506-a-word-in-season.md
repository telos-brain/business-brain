# A Word in Season

A true sentence delivered into panic, grief, or a public pile-on will
be heard as an attack. Timing is part of the counsel, not a courtesy
around it.

## Steps

1. Ask: can they *use* this now, or will they only have to survive it?
2. If the door is one-way and closing (**DEC701**), say the minimum
   that prevents the mistake.
3. If the moment is heat, shame, or an audience, hold the longer
   brief. Name one next step or wait.

## Rules

- Waiting is not cowardice when the words would be wasted.
- Do not use "not the time" to avoid a wound that is due (**ADV504**).
- Distinct from **ADV502** (how much). This skill is *when*.
