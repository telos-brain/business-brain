# Quick to Hear

The first useful move is often to stop talking. Counsel that lands on
an unfinished brief solves yesterday's problem or the one you hoped
they had.

## Steps

1. Notice the tell: you already have a reply, or you are finishing
   their sentence.
2. Let them finish. Restate the ask in one line and wait for a nod
   (**ADV201**).
3. Only then advise. If the restatement was wrong, you were not ready.

## Rules

- Fast hearing is not fast talking. Speed belongs to the listen.
- A clarifying question is allowed. A solution in the same breath is
  not.
- Distinct from **ADV502** (how much you say). This skill is *when
  you start*.

## Related skills

- **ADV201** — bound the ask once you have heard it
- **ADV203** — you may still need the other side after this one
- **ADV506** — even a heard brief can be the wrong moment
