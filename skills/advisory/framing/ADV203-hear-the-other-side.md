# Hear the Other Side

A single telling is a case, not a picture. The first account is usually
coherent because the teller has already chosen what matters. The missing
side is often the constraint that would change the advice.

## Steps

1. Name whose account you have, and whose you do not.
2. Restate the first telling in one or two lines (**ADV501**).
3. Ask what the other side would say, or get it. Do not invent their
   motive — get the facts they would add.
4. Only then advise. If you still have one telling, say so and bound
   the advice as provisional.

## Rules

- Speed is not an excuse when the other side is one conversation away.
- Hearing them is not taking their side. It is refusing a half-brief.
- Distinct from **ADV201** (what the ask is). This skill is whether
  you have *both* accounts of it.

## Related skills

- **ADV501** — steelman the first telling before you seek the second
- **ADV507** — finish listening once they are in the room
- **DEC401** — the other side may be a blind spot, not a person
