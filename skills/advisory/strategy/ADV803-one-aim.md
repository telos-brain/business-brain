# One Aim

A plan that tries to please two opposed ends will serve neither. You
will look busy and still miss. Instability is not a lack of effort. It
is two aims wearing one name.

## Steps

1. Write the aims you are actually serving — not the slogan, the
   outcomes you would count as a win.
2. If two of them fight (speed vs quality, this customer vs that
   standard, growth vs the name), say so.
3. Pick one for *this* decision, or split them into two decisions.
   Do not leave both in the same sentence.

## Rules

- "Both" is allowed only when they do not trade off in this move.
- A second aim that you will not name will still steer you.
- Distinct from **ADV801** (what to stop). This skill is *which
  outcome the remaining work is for*.
