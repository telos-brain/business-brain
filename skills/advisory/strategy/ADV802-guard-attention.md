# Guard Attention

You become what you look at. A day spent in other people's panic, feeds,
or deals will produce their priorities, not yours. Attention is the
budget behind every other budget.

## Steps

1. Name what has been filling the last stretch of attention (channels,
   people, metrics, a grievance).
2. Ask: is this input serving the open decision, or is it borrowing
   the mind?
3. Cut or time-box the input that is not paying rent. Return to the
   one thing that would change the outcome (**ADV202**).

## Rules

- Guarding attention is not ignorance. Pull a fact when you need it
  (**DEC702**), then close the tap.
- Envy is an attention leak with a moral costume.
- Distinct from **ADV801** (what work to stop). This skill is what you
  *let in*.
