# Fear of a Person Is a Snare

A person can be a fact (they decide, they pay, they can fire you).
Fear of them is something else — it edits the advice before the
evidence does. That is a snare. You will call it tact and mean
self-protection.

## Steps

1. Write the advice you would give if this person could not punish
   or reward you.
2. Write the advice you are about to give.
3. If they differ, name the fear (access, status, temper, the next
   meeting). Put the first version back on the table, then decide
   what you will actually say (**ADV503**, **ADV506**).

## Rules

- Softening the *tone* is allowed. Softening the *point* to keep
  a person sweet is the snare.
- "They will not like it" is not a reason the plan is good.
- Distinct from **ADV505** (whether they will use the advice). This
  skill is whether *their power* is writing it.
